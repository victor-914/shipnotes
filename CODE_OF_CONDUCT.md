# Code of Conduct

shipnotes follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short version. The linked text is the full policy.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in shipnotes to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the people who use shipnotes.

## Not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to the maintainer privately, through the contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/victor-914). You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on any comment. Reports are handled in confidence.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code. They may also warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
